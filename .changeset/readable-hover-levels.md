Readable hover now works together with readable text: hovered text is shown one level larger than the readable text level (large when readable text is off or normal, extra large when it is large or extra large). With readable text off, hovered elements are never enlarged more than with readable text normal. The readable hover and readable text buttons in the view header are easier to tell apart from off in dark mode.
